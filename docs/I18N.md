# Internationalization

Moderaty currently supports English (`en`) and Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`) as
first-class locale values. On bilingual paths, locale selection uses the
`moderaty_locale` HttpOnly cookie; a visitor without that cookie is resolved
from `Accept-Language`, with English as the fail-closed default. The server
rewrites the document `lang` attribute for dynamic HTML responses.

The language switcher posts to `/api/locale`. The endpoint accepts only the two
supported locales and only same-site relative return paths. It sets a one-year
`SameSite=Lax` cookie and redirects back to the page that initiated the change.

The runtime bilingual allowlist is exactly `/login` and `/account-deleted`.
Those are the only routes that resolve the saved/browser locale; all other
surfaces, including the landing, signed-in app, maintenance, and consent,
resolve to English. New UI strings must be added to both entries in
`src/lib/i18n/messages.ts`; `t()` throws when a catalog entry is missing.

## Legal and marketing publication gate

The Terms, Privacy Policy, and DPA are legally controlled documents. Their
current published bodies remain English until a qualified Portuguese legal
translation is reviewed together with the real legal identity, CNPJ, address,
processor disclosures, consent wording, and `LEGAL_VERSION` bump. The app must
not claim that a Portuguese legal version is published before that review.

A Portuguese legal translation must be published as a complete, versioned
bundle. Do not translate individual clauses in isolation. Update the consent
checkbox text and its exact persisted evidence in the same change, then extend
`src/lib/landing/legal.test.ts` to pin the Portuguese bundle and bump
`LEGAL_VERSION`.
